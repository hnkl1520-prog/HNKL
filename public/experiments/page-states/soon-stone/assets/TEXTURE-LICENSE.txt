MAPPING NOTES
Use repeating wrap. Start around 1.5–2 repeats per visible letter face and maintain consistent world-space texture density between letters.
The original photographed surface is approximately 1.8 m tall per tile.
The source has a pale sandy-gray mineral tint. Any artistic brightening/desaturation should happen in the material rather than modifying these source files.
Add the AO map only when the mesh has the appropriate UV coordinates for the renderer.
